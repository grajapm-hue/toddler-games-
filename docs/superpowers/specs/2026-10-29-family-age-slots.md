Approved in chat: parents may choose ages 2, 3, 4 or 5 as convenient starting sets, never ability grades. All five existing games and Explore / Play / Challenge remain available within every age. Keep encouragement and educational counting; do not add rankings, failure labels, required scores or performance-based age assignment. Future games can be inserted later.

The welcome offers four large parent-facing age buttons. The game-menu header offers a compact parent age selector; age controls are absent from game rooms. Keep the accepted visual portrait welcome, landscape game menu/rooms, camera margins and fixed-width sliding game cards. Update age-range copy to 2–5 and replace the Play subtitle with guided play.

Implementation choices: age selection is optional, preserving existing common play for families who have not chosen. No date of birth, account or new data collection. A chosen age is saved locally. Each age remembers its own level and explicit bubble settings; new age slots start in Explore. Larger/slower objects and fewer simultaneous tasks are starting settings, not restrictions. Full number/letter/shape bubble sets remain available through an explicit Full set toggle. The abacus retains all 100 beads and unrestricted movement; its three sub-levels supply optional counting suggestions, never completion gates.

Use a shared profile registry for both welcome and play. Keep existing common-play settings compatible. Parent selection should still work for the current session if storage is blocked; navigation must carry the chosen age without depending on storage. A different tab changing age must not interrupt a running game.

Publish Beta v0.30/cache v30, update the editable prototype and self-contained build, and precache every new runtime asset. Preserve manifest identity, scope, display mode and orientation. No new game or native app package is part of this change.
